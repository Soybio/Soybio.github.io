Datasets

Abiotic stress RNA-seq expression

Expression of 52,673 soybean genes in 192 public RNA-seq samples from 14 studies of salt, drought and heat stress, all processed in the same way against the Wm82.a4 reference. Includes differential expression results for 30 stress-versus-control comparisons.

Plot genes Dataset details and downloads

ZH13 to Wm82.a4 gene ID conversion

A one-to-one gene ID table between the soybean genomes Zhonghuang 13 (ZH13) and Williams 82 assembly 4 (Wm82.a4). Each gene pair has a confidence level (High, Medium or No correspondence) and a short note on the evidence. Wm82.a2.v1 gene IDs are also given.
