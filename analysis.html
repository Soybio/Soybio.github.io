Methods

  • Annotation: GO and KEGG annotations of Wm82.a4 genes; Pfam domains of Wm82.a2.v1 gene models, converted to Wm82.a4 IDs with the gene ID table. Download the files on the annotation dataset page.
  • Enrichment test: one-sided hypergeometric test (over-representation) with Benjamini–Hochberg correction (FDR). Only genes with at least one annotation of the tested type are counted. Terms with fewer than 5 genes in the background, and KEGG global maps such as "Metabolic pathways", are not tested.
  • Stress response: a gene is salt-, drought- or heat-responsive if it is differentially expressed (adjusted p < 0.05 and |log2 fold change| ≥ 1) in at least one comparison of that stress. The background is all 52,673 genes in the RNA-seq data.
  • Co-expression: genes with mean TPM ≥ 2. For each gene, log2(TPM + 1) values were centred within each study and tissue, so that the correlation reflects shared responses to stress treatments rather than tissue or study differences. 33,357 genes are included.
