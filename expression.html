Gene Expression under Abiotic Stress

Expression (TPM) of soybean genes in 192 public RNA-seq samples from 14 studies of salt, drought and heat stress. Enter one gene to see a bar plot, or several genes to see a heatmap.

Wm82.a4, ZH13 and Wm82.a2.v1 gene IDs are accepted; ZH13 and Wm82.a2.v1 IDs are converted to Wm82.a4 automatically. Enter one gene for a bar plot, or 2 to 200 genes for a heatmap.

About the data

  • The data are 192 public RNA-seq samples from 14 NCBI BioProjects: 104 salt, 66 drought and 22 heat stress samples, including their controls. Leaf, root and pod tissues are included.
  • All samples were processed in the same way against the Williams 82 reference genome (Wm82.a4.v1). Expression is given as TPM (transcripts per million).
  • In bar plots, each bar is the mean of the biological replicates and the error bar is the standard deviation. Dots are individual samples.
  • In heatmaps, "Z-score per gene" scales each gene to mean 0 and standard deviation 1 across the shown columns, which makes patterns easy to compare between genes.
  • Full details, the list of studies and the download files are on the dataset page.
