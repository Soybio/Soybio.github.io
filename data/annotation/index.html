Functional annotation (GO, KEGG, Pfam)

Gene Ontology, KEGG pathway and Pfam domain annotation of soybean Williams 82 genes (Wm82.a4 gene IDs). These files are used by the Gene Analysis page.

Summary

AnnotationAnnotated genesTermsNotes
GO Biological Process20,8327,97124,191 genes have at least one GO term. Parent terms are included for every gene.
GO Molecular Function17,9982,606
GO Cellular Component20,6131,246
KEGG pathways11,6441352,252 KEGG orthology (KO) groups. 7 global maps (such as "Metabolic pathways") are not used in enrichment tests.
Pfam domains39,4474,183Annotated on Wm82.a2.v1 gene models (45,533 genes) and converted to Wm82.a4 IDs with the gene ID table.

Notes

  • Genes without any annotation of a type are not counted in enrichment tests for that type, so results are not biased by unannotated genes.
  • About 6,100 Pfam-annotated Wm82.a2.v1 genes have no matching Wm82.a4 gene in the gene ID table and are not included.
