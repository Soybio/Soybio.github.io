ZH13 to Wm82.a4 gene ID conversion

One-to-one gene ID table between the soybean genomes Zhonghuang 13 (ZH13) and Williams 82 assembly 4 (Wm82.a4), in both directions.

Overview

This table gives, for each ZH13 gene, the matching Wm82.a4 gene, and for each Wm82.a4 gene, the matching ZH13 gene. Gene pairs were identified by synteny with MCscan and checked with a whole-genome liftover of ZH13 genes onto Wm82.a4. Each pair has a confidence level and a short evidence note. The results were also compared with the gene ID converter of SoyOD.

Confidence levels

The confidence level depends on two kinds of evidence: synteny (MCscan) and whole-genome liftover. The Evidence column gives the exact reason for each gene.

Confidence Evidence ZH13 genes
High Syntenic ortholog (MCscan anchor), confirmed by whole-genome liftover 38,588
Syntenic ortholog (MCscan lifted anchor), confirmed by whole-genome liftover 3,972
Medium Syntenic ortholog, but the liftover lands on a different Wm82.a4 gene (usually a neighbouring tandem copy) 436
Syntenic ortholog, but the liftover finds no Wm82.a4 gene at this locus 352
No correspondence No syntenic ortholog; the locus aligns to Wm82.a4, but no Wm82.a4 gene is annotated there 9,893
No syntenic ortholog; the liftover overlaps a Wm82.a4 gene, but MCscan finds no syntenic support 1,310
No syntenic ortholog; only part of the CDS aligns to Wm82.a4 (gene absent or highly divergent in Wm82.a4) 892
  • MCscan anchor: the gene pair was found directly in a synteny block.
  • MCscan lifted anchor: the gene pair was added by MCscan inside an existing synteny block.
  • Medium pairs are supported by synteny, but the liftover does not agree. Please check these pairs if the gene is important for your work, especially in tandem gene families.

How to use

  • Online: paste your gene IDs into the Gene ID Converter.
  • In Excel: open the Excel file, choose the sheet for the direction you need, and search with Find (Ctrl+F).
  • In R, Python or on the command line: use the TSV files, as in the examples below.

R

zh2wm <- read.delim("ZH13_to_Wm82a4.tsv", check.names = FALSE)
my_genes <- c("SoyZH13_01G000024", "SoyZH13_01G029800")
subset(zh2wm, ZH13_gene_ID %in% my_genes)[, c("ZH13_gene_ID", "Wm82.a4_gene_ID", "Confidence")]

Python

import pandas as pd

zh2wm = pd.read_csv("ZH13_to_Wm82a4.tsv", sep="\t")
my_genes = ["SoyZH13_01G000024", "SoyZH13_01G029800"]
zh2wm[zh2wm["ZH13_gene_ID"].isin(my_genes)][["ZH13_gene_ID", "Wm82.a4_gene_ID", "Confidence"]]

Command line

grep -w "SoyZH13_01G000024" ZH13_to_Wm82a4.tsv | cut -f 1-3
