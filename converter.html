Gene ID Converter

Convert soybean gene IDs between ZH13 and Wm82.a4. You can also enter Wm82.a2.v1 gene IDs.

Enter one ID per line, or separate IDs with spaces or commas. Transcript and protein suffixes (for example .1, .m1, .1.p) are removed automatically. Up to 20,000 IDs at a time.

Notes

  • With Detect automatically, IDs starting with SoyZH13_ are read as ZH13 IDs, and IDs starting with Glyma. are read as Wm82.a4 IDs. If a Glyma. ID is not a Wm82.a4 gene, it is looked up as a Wm82.a2.v1 ID.
  • Wm82.a2.v1 IDs are converted through the matching Wm82.a4 gene.
  • For the meaning of High, Medium and No correspondence, see Confidence levels.
  • The converter uses the ZH13 to Wm82.a4 gene ID conversion table (version 1.0, October 2026). It runs in your browser; your gene lists are not sent anywhere.
