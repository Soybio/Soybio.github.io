Resources

Databases and software that are useful for soybean genomics. All links open the official websites.

Soybean databases

NameDescriptionWebsite
SoyBase USDA-ARS database for soybean genetics and genomics: genomes, genes, genetic maps and QTLs soybase.org
SoyOmics Soybean multi-omics database at the China National Center for Bioinformation (CNCB-NGDC) ngdc.cncb.ac.cn/soyomics
SoyOD Soybean omics database, including a gene ID converter between soybean genomes bis.zju.edu.cn/soyod
Legume Information System (LIS) Genomic data and tools for legume species, including soybean legumeinfo.org

Genome portals and data repositories

NameDescriptionWebsite
Phytozome Plant genome portal of the DOE Joint Genome Institute; provides Williams 82 genome assemblies and annotations phytozome-next.jgi.doe.gov
Ensembl Plants Genome browser and gene annotation for Glycine max plants.ensembl.org
NCBI Datasets Soybean genome assemblies and annotations at NCBI ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/datasets
Genome Warehouse (GWH) Genome assembly repository at CNCB-NGDC ngdc.cncb.ac.cn/gwh

Gene function and sequence search

NameDescriptionWebsite
UniProt Protein sequences and functional information uniprot.org
InterPro Protein families, domains and Gene Ontology terms ebi.ac.uk/interpro
NCBI BLAST Online search for similar DNA and protein sequences blast.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov

Software for comparative genomics

SoftwareUsed forWebsite
JCVI / MCscan Synteny analysis and synteny plots between genomes github.com/tanghaibao/jcvi
Liftoff Transferring gene annotations from one genome assembly to another github.com/agshumate/Liftoff
minimap2 Aligning DNA sequences, including whole genomes and long reads github.com/lh3/minimap2
DIAMOND Fast protein sequence search (BLAST-compatible) github.com/bbuchfink/diamond
OrthoFinder Finding orthologous genes and gene families across species github.com/davidemms/OrthoFinder
SAMtools Working with sequence alignment files (SAM, BAM, CRAM) htslib.org
BEDTools Comparing and combining genomic intervals (BED, GFF, VCF) bedtools.readthedocs.io
