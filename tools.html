Choose a tool from the list. Each tool has an example that can be loaded with one click. Plots can be saved as SVG or PNG and tables as CSV.

Accuracy

The constants used by the tools (amino acid masses, pK values, nearest-neighbour parameters, the genetic code, BLOSUM62 and REBASE enzyme data) are taken from Biopython. The results were compared with Biopython and SciPy in more than 4,500 test cases and agree in all of them. Where a calculation follows a published method, the method and its reference are given with the tool.

Privacy

All calculations run in your web browser. Sequences and data you paste are not uploaded or stored.

Protein properties

Physical and chemical parameters of a protein, as in ExPASy ProtParam, with an amino acid composition chart and a hydropathy plot.

Methods

Molecular weight from average residue masses; theoretical pI with the pK values of Bjellqvist et al. (as ExPASy); extinction coefficients by Pace et al. (1995); instability index by Guruprasad et al. (1990), above 40 = unstable; aliphatic index by Ikai (1980); GRAVY and hydropathy by Kyte & Doolittle (1982); aromaticity by Lobry (1994). Results match Biopython ProtParam.

DNA statistics

Base composition, GC content along the sequence and the reverse complement.

ORF finder and translation

Finds open reading frames (ATG to stop codon) in all six frames and translates the sequence in a chosen frame (standard genetic code).

Codon usage

Codon counts, relative synonymous codon usage (RSCU) and GC content at the three codon positions of a coding sequence.

Pairwise alignment

Optimal alignment of two sequences with affine gap penalties: global (Needleman–Wunsch) or local (Smith–Waterman).

Methods

Dynamic programming with affine gaps (Gotoh 1982); a gap of length k costs open + (k − 1) × extend, as in EMBOSS and Biopython. Scores match Biopython PairwiseAligner. Identity and similarity are given as a percentage of the alignment length. Up to about 2,000 × 2,000 residues.

Primer check and in-silico PCR

Melting temperature, GC content, 3′ end and dimer checks for a primer pair, and the PCR products on a template sequence.

Restriction sites

Cut positions and fragment sizes for common restriction enzymes (REBASE data), a restriction map, and the enzymes that do not cut, for choosing cloning sites.

Enzymes ·
Methods

Recognition sites and cut positions from REBASE (via Biopython), searched on both strands, so Type IIS enzymes such as BsaI, BbsI, BsmBI and SapI are found in either orientation. Cut positions match Biopython Restriction.search for linear DNA.

qPCR relative expression

Fold change of a target gene relative to a control group, normalised to one or more reference genes. Use the Reference Gene Finder to choose stable reference genes.

Amplification factor 2 = 100% efficiency (the 2−ΔΔCt method). Enter measured values (for example 1.95) from the standard curve tool to use the Pfaffl method.

qPCR standard curve and primer efficiency

Amplification efficiency of a primer pair from a dilution series.
